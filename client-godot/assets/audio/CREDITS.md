# Audio credits

All sound effects are by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl), released under **CC0 1.0**
(public domain, no attribution required; credited here anyway).
The music is by **RandomMind** on OpenGameArt (**CC0 1.0**) and by
**Kevin MacLeod** (incompetech.com, **CC BY 4.0**). Kevin MacLeod's pieces must
stay credited in the game (Settings shows the credits line):

> "Suonatore di Liuto", "Village Consort", "Midnight Tale" Kevin MacLeod (incompetech.com)
> Licensed under Creative Commons: By Attribution 4.0 License
> http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Files are copied unchanged from the packs (renamed by meaning), except the
music, which was trimmed / loudness-levelled and re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis
(stereo, 44.1 kHz, about 95-110 kbps). The newer pieces (`music_*.ogg`)
are levelled to -21.6 LUFS integrated, the same as game_suonatore_di_liuto.ogg;
scripts/sfx.gd sets each piece's volume in its playlist.
