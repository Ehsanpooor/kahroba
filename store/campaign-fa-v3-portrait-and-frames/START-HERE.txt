HOW TO ADD YOUR SCREENSHOT
1. Open the PNG overlay in an editor that supports layers.
2. Add your own game screenshot as a separate layer UNDER the overlay.
3. Scale and position the screenshot to cover the entire transparent opening.
4. Export the combined image as PNG or JPEG.
Do not flatten the overlay before adding your screenshot. Black seen in some previews is the display background behind the transparent hole.
The openings have been checked for actual alpha-zero pixels, not painted checkerboards.
files-and-placement.json includes canvas sizes and opening bounds for easier positioning.
